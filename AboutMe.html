About me

More than just pixels on a screen.

Hi, I'm Rohit — a UI/UX Designer who cares more about whether an interface works than whether it wins an award. I believe good design is largely invisible: it just gets out of the way and lets people do what they came to do.

I come from a B.Sc. Computer Science background, which shapes how I approach design — structurally, with an eye for logic, edge cases, and how a screen actually gets built. Whether I'm architecting a design system in Figma, wireframing a flow, or tuning a micro-interaction, the goal is the same: interfaces that are intuitive, accessible, and a little delightful.
