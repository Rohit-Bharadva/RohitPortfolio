Mealora

Smart grocery shopping and meal planning experience.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

2 Weeks

Platform

Mobile App

Tools

Figma, Prototyping

Seamless planning

Users needed a way to bridge the gap between finding a recipe and actually buying the ingredients. The traditional process was broken — it meant switching between a recipe app, a notes app, and a grocery app just to get dinner on the table.

I designed a unified flow where meal planning feeds directly into the grocery cart, so choosing a recipe automatically builds the shopping list behind it. That cut the number of decisions a user has to make in half, and kept the whole journey inside one app.

Car Rental App

A seamless car rental experience with real-time availability.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

2 Weeks

Platform

Mobile App

Tools

Figma, Prototyping

Connecting renters & owners

A peer-to-peer car rental platform that lets owners monetize their vehicles while giving renters access to affordable, varied options. The design had to serve two very different user groups without feeling like two different apps.

By building distinct, intuitive flows for renters booking a car and owners listing one — while sharing the same visual language — I created a trusted marketplace that simplifies rental decisions and keeps vehicles in use rather than parked.

Fitness Buddy

A fitness companion app for personalized workouts and meal planning.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

2 Weeks

Platform

Mobile App

Tools

Figma, Prototyping

Actionable health data

Health tracking can be overwhelming fast. Users didn't need another spreadsheet of numbers — they needed to glance at their phone mid-workout and instantly understand where they stood.

I stripped the dashboard down to the handful of metrics that actually change behaviour, and used high-contrast accents so key numbers stay legible even outdoors, mid-set, out of breath.

Bridging the gap
